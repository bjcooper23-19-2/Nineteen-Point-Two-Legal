WAIA Subprocessor List

Third-party providers used to host, operate, support, secure and improve WAIA where they may process customer personal data.

Proposed: 14 October 2026 Version: 1.2 draft

Nineteen Point Two uses selected third-party providers to provide, host, secure, support and operate WAIA.

Where these providers process Customer Personal Data on behalf of Nineteen Point Two, they are treated as subprocessors.

The current WAIA source and operations runbook identify the following processing routes. Their exact live deployment, contracting chain and transfer arrangements must be verified before this draft is adopted as a customer-facing subprocessor register.

WAIA processing routes for verification

  • Lovable: WAIA source includes Lovable service routes and prior procurement material describes Lovable Cloud hosting. Its current production hosting role and contractual relationship need operator confirmation.
  • Supabase: WAIA source uses Supabase-backed authentication, database and storage functions for customer and learner records. Whether Supabase is a direct supplier or sits in Lovable’s underlying chain needs confirmation.
  • Resend: the WAIA transactional email delivery runbook records Resend as the production sender for account and invite emails. This supersedes the previous statement that Resend is merely source support. Confirm live activation, data fields, agreement and transfer position before finalising its subprocessor entry.

Other supplier roles

Cloudflare Web Analytics measures public WAIA website use. Google Search Console supports search performance. Tally receives public enquiries and their submitted attribution context. These are website or enquiry services, not WAIA learner-data subprocessors on the evidence reviewed. Business email, document, finance and professional-adviser suppliers serve ordinary business operations and must be assessed separately for any Customer Personal Data they actually receive.

WAIA source reviewed for this draft does not establish an active AI model provider receiving learner personal data. A future feature that changes this would require a fresh supplier and DPA review.

Subprocessor updates

Nineteen Point Two may update this list as providers are added, removed or replaced.

Where required by the Data Processing Agreement, customers will be given notice of material subprocessor changes and the opportunity to object on reasonable data protection grounds.
