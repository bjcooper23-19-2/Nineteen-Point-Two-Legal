Cookie Notice

How Nineteen Point Two Limited uses cookies and similar technologies on the website and WAIA product.

Proposed: 14 October 2026 Version: 1.2 draft

This Cookie Notice explains how Nineteen Point Two uses cookies and similar technologies on its website and WAIA product.

1. What cookies are

Cookies are small files placed on your device when you visit a website or use an online service. Similar technologies may include local storage, pixels, tags, scripts and device identifiers.

These technologies can help a site work properly, remember preferences, support security, measure usage or support marketing activity.

3. Strictly necessary cookies

Strictly necessary cookies are used to provide services you have requested, such as secure login, authentication, account access and platform security.

These cookies cannot usually be switched off because the service would not work properly without them.

4. Analytics cookies

The WAIA marketing website includes a Cloudflare Web Analytics beacon. It measures page loads and aggregate website use, including visits to first-party routes that hand visitors to the enquiry form. Cloudflare describes this service as cookieless, but the beacon’s operation and any access to device information still need a PECR assessment.

Google Search Console is used to understand search visibility and performance. The marketing repository does not show a Search Console browser tag. It is distinct from Cloudflare’s website analytics beacon.

The legal basis and any consent or objection control for the Cloudflare beacon must be settled against the live configuration before this draft is published.

5. WAIA enquiry source attribution

WAIA marketing pages use sessionStorage to retain an approved short enquiry source code during a browser session. They may also retain a bounded content code supplied in the URL. The site uses those values to route a visitor to the Tally enquiry form and pass the source and content context. The site code does not store a name, email address, page history or timestamp in this storage. Tally processes the information a visitor chooses to submit in the form, together with the passed enquiry context.

Browser session storage falls within the rules for storage and access technologies. The applicable PECR consent or exception position, including any required objection mechanism, remains for legal and implementation review.

6. Marketing cookies

Marketing cookies may be used to understand campaign performance or support relevant advertising.

Current marketing cookies or pixels: We do not currently use marketing cookies or advertising pixels as far as currently known.

7. Managing cookies

No cookie banner or settings control has been verified for the current WAIA marketing website. Where consent or a right to object is required for the technologies described above, the website must provide an effective control before relying on it.

You can also control cookies through your browser settings. Blocking some cookies may affect how the website or WAIA product works.

8. Changes to this Cookie Notice

We may update this Cookie Notice from time to time. The latest version will be published on our website.
