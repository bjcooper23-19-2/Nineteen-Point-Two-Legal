Cookie Notice

How Nineteen Point Two Limited uses cookies and similar technologies on the website and WAIA product.

Last updated: 15 October 2026 Version: 1.3

This Cookie Notice explains how Nineteen Point Two uses cookies and similar technologies on its website and WAIA product.

1. What cookies are

Cookies are small files placed on your device when you visit a website or use an online service. Similar technologies may include local storage, pixels, tags, scripts and device identifiers.

These technologies can help a site work properly, remember preferences, support security, measure usage or support marketing activity.

3. Strictly necessary cookies

Strictly necessary cookies are used to provide services you have requested, such as secure login, authentication, account access and platform security.

These cookies cannot usually be switched off because the service would not work properly without them.

4. Analytics cookies

The WAIA marketing website uses a manually installed Cloudflare Web Analytics beacon to measure page loads and website use. Cloudflare describes the service as cookieless. The website disables automatic Cloudflare snippet injection.

You can object to this analytics use at analytics preferences. The choice is saved in first-party localStorage so it applies on later visits from that browser. Clearing browser storage removes the saved choice.

Google Search Console separately helps us understand search visibility. It does not require a Search Console browser tag on the WAIA site.

5. Source attribution, Visibility Check and forms

A valid source code in a direct WAIA link can be passed to a form without being stored in the browser. Marketing attribution does not use sessionStorage. Visibility Check results are held in sessionStorage within the browser tab so you can view them during that tab session.

Tally forms load after you choose to open them. Tally may use its own storage and access technologies when the form opens; its notice explains those technologies. Contact Us and Visibility Check do not save partial submissions.

6. Managing browser storage

Use the analytics preferences page linked above to object to Cloudflare Web Analytics. You can also manage stored data and cookies through your browser settings. Blocking some cookies may affect how the website or WAIA product works.

7. Changes to this Cookie Notice

We may update this Cookie Notice from time to time. The latest version will be published on our website.
