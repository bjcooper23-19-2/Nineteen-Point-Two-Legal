2. Our role under data protection law

Our role depends on the context.

Where we are controller

We are controller where we decide why and how personal data is used. This includes personal data used for:

  • our website
  • sales and marketing
  • prospecting and business development
  • customer relationship management
  • billing and account administration
  • support enquiries
  • supplier management
  • our own business operations

Where we are processor

For Customer Personal Data provided or managed by a customer organisation through WAIA, including learner records, organisational baselines, application evidence and management review, the customer is controller and Nineteen Point Two acts as processor under the Data Processing Agreement.

The customer decides why this data is used, who should access WAIA, what learning is assigned, and how WAIA records are used internally. Nineteen Point Two processes it under the customer’s documented instructions. We remain controller for our own customer contacts, billing, website enquiries and business operations.

We use cookies and similar technologies where needed to operate our website or platform, remember preferences, maintain security, understand usage or support analytics.

Depending on the circumstances, you may have rights to:

  • correct inaccurate personal data
  • request deletion
  • restrict processing
  • object to processing
  • request portability
  • withdraw consent where processing is based on consent
  • complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office

To exercise a right, email ben@nineteenpointtwo.com and tell us what you want to do. We may ask for information needed to confirm your identity. We will respond within the period required by law and explain any applicable exception. You can object to processing based on legitimate interests and withdraw consent at any time where consent is the basis; withdrawal does not affect earlier lawful processing.

Where your data is processed in WAIA on behalf of your employer or organisation, we will direct your request to that controller or assist it as required by the Data Processing Agreement.

If you remain unhappy, you can complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office at ico.org.uk.
