# WAIA legal reconciliation: release review

**15 October 2026. Internal review record for draft PR #2. Do not merge or publish before the release conditions below are resolved.**

## Verified basis

- WAIA runs on Lovable Cloud. Lovable manages the underlying Supabase infrastructure. The production database is in Europe (Stockholm). Row-level security is active on all 36 public tables. Organisation administrators and learners have organisation-scoped access; platform administrators have intentional cross-organisation access. The reviewed production policies showed no material cross-organisation access gap.
- Daily database backups extend back approximately 14 days. Point-in-time recovery is unavailable and restoration was not tested in this review. Customer export and complete organisation deletion are manual operations that must be validated for each request.
- The product repository merged customer offboarding and security incident response playbooks through PR #345. Those procedures do not establish automated or fully tested export, deletion or restoration.
- Lovable provides managed hosting, backend infrastructure and authentication emails. Cloudflare supports hosting and edge infrastructure and separately provides website analytics. Resend sends invitations, learner nudges and administrator digests. Its account-generated signed DPA identifies US primary processing and applicable EU SCCs and UK transfer provisions. Resend's account termination deletion period is not a WAIA customer offboarding promise.
- Tally hosts Contact Us and Visibility Check. Neither saves partial submissions. Contact Us has no external integration. Visibility Check has an enabled Google Apps Script webhook for its Google-based access process.
- Marketing draft PR #87 installs the Cloudflare analytics snippet manually, disables automatic snippet injection, provides `/analytics-preferences/` from public footers and stores objections in first-party `localStorage` under `waia:analytics-objection`. Marketing attribution does not use `sessionStorage`; valid direct-link source attribution remains. Visibility Check results use tab `sessionStorage`. Tally forms load following user action. The Visibility Check Tally privacy link points to the legal Privacy Notice. PR #87 is draft and undeployed.
- No customer-data AI model processing was identified in the current application code.

## Publication conditions

1. **PECR decision:** A solicitor must determine whether the configured Cloudflare beacon involves storage or access on a visitor's device; if so, whether its actual sole purpose, data fields, aggregation and retention satisfy the statistical-purposes exception, including clear information and an easy, effective objection. If the exception does not apply, the marketing site needs valid prior consent and blocking until consent. The solicitor should also confirm the basis for the Visibility Check tab storage and the objection preference storage. The legal notices must be aligned with that decision. The current text makes no conclusive exception claim.
2. **Coordinated release:** Marketing PR #87 must be approved and deployed with the legal notices. Until then, the notices describe controls and storage behaviour that are not live. Check the live Cloudflare account setting, the public footer links, the objection control and both Tally form routes before publication.
3. **Contract approval:** The reviewed Customer Order Form incorporates the Terms and DPA by URL. The versions published at those URLs when both parties sign apply; Nineteen Point Two retains copies with the signed Order Form and provides them on request. No separate version-number fields are required. Confirm the remaining commercial particulars and obtain targeted solicitor review of DPA audit, transfer, backup and return/deletion clauses; website liability wording; and the relationship between the DPA and SaaS Terms.

## Canonical WAIA Terms and commercial follow-up

The canonical paid SaaS Terms remain in the separate marketing repository and were read from its current `terms/index.html` on 15 October 2026. They have **not** been changed here. The following contradictions need a separate marketing Terms amendment before using the agreed Customer Order Form model:

| Location              | Current conflict                                                                                    | Required alignment                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| --------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Opening, sections 1–2 | “Workplace AI Academy”, package-based service scope, broad order-form/proposal references           | Name the single WAIA product and make the signed Customer Order Form the paid contract entry point; use its URL and signature-date version rule.                                                    |
| Section 8             | Billing frequency, invoice-date access start, unspecified renewal terms and additional-fee language | Fixed term and start date in the Order Form; one upfront invoice for subscription and any scoped implementation; 30-day payment default unless the Order Form says otherwise; no automatic renewal. |
| Sections 12 and 25    | Broad Customer Data licence and post-termination retention for “legitimate business needs”          | Preserve ownership and necessary service licence, but make Customer Personal Data use and return/deletion subject to the DPA; remove the broad retention exception for that data.                   |
| Sections 16–17        | DPA incorporation and subprocessor terms do not consistently refer to a signed Order Form           | Align the operative DPA version and DPA priority for Customer Personal Data.                                                                                                                        |
| Section 19            | Future AI processing wording is broad                                                               | Keep the existing no-intentional-model-training protection; clarify any future material processing through the DPA and supplier change process if needed.                                           |
| Section 23            | Liability cap looks back to fees paid or payable in the preceding 12 months                         | Solicitor to assess its fit and enforceability with an upfront fixed-term invoice. Do not change the allocation without advice.                                                                     |
| Sections 24–25        | No explicit no-automatic-renewal rule and generic export/retention language                         | State that the fixed term ends unless extended by signed agreement; align manual export/deletion and backup handling with the DPA.                                                                  |

Existing customer accountability for tool selection, permitted AI use, human review and workplace decisions should be retained. The marketing pricing page also needs a separate commercial review where it refers to included implementation, annual bands and Guided Adoption pricing. The reviewed Customer Order Form requires no separate document version-number fields. Its signature-date rule and retained archival copies control which published Terms and DPA versions apply.
